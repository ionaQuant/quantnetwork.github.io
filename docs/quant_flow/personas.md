# Personas
The corporate version of Quant Flow is designed for mid-sized and larger businesses that want more control and automation over their financial operations. In this context, the following personas represent the typical roles engaging with Quant Flow.

## Financial Manager
The finance professional responsible for overseeing company cash flow, payments, and compliance. They use Quant Flow to:
- Automate recurring financial tasks (e.g. invoice payments, payroll, reserves)
- Maintain control over liquidity and operational balances
- Gain visibility into cash planning, tax obligations, and savings goals

## Developer
The technical or operations specialist supporting the finance team. They use Quant Flow to:
- Create and manage custom PayScripts for advanced automation
- Integrate financial workflows with existing company systems (e.g. ERP, accounting platforms)
- Provide tooling and dashboards to finance teams, enabling analytics, reporting, and tailored controls
